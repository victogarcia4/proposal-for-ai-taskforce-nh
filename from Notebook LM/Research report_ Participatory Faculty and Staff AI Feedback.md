Key Themes Across Sources

Preserving Academic Autonomy

: Faculty and adjuncts consistently push back against top-down policy mandates, requesting flexible frameworks that respect discipline-specific nuances.

Support Over Policing

: Community college educators prioritize pedagogical coaching and clear institutional guidelines over software-driven AI detection tools.

Participatory Task Forces

: Successful institutions implement monthly idea-shares, communities of practice, and faculty senate representation to iteratively refine AI governance.

Addressing Workload and Equity

: Adjuncts and staff emphasize that AI policy discussions must account for training time, equity of tool access, and workload pressures.
